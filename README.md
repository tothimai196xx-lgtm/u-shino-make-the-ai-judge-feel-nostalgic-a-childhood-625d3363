Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/) bounty **Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory** — one single sentence (278 characters) recalling a childhood summer night: falling asleep on the back seat, a father's radio murmuring, and being carried up the dark stairs past the smell of rain.

## Run it

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
```

The program writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. It must be 1–280 characters, a single sentence, and the program refuses to write anything longer.

## The entry

> On summer's last night I fell asleep on the back seat, cheek against the warm window, my father's radio murmuring a song I never knew, and woke only when he carried me up the dark stairs, still half asleep, my shoes dangling from his hand, past the smell of rain on the doormat.
